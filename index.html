Statistical Findings & Interpretation

Holding spatial constraints constant, our log-log model demonstrates a statistically significant relationship ($p < 0.001$). A 1% increase in a nation's GDP per capita yields a corresponding increase in infrastructure observation visibility, validating our initial structural hypotheses.
